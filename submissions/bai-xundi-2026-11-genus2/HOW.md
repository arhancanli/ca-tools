Construction: genus-2 two-block covering arrays, Theorem 5.2 of Bai XunDi, "Two-block covering arrays from elliptic curves and curves of genus two" (preprint, 8 November 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24055371). Take a curve X: y^2 = f(x) of genus 2 over F_q (q prime here: 7, 11, 19) with two rational points at infinity, the space V = L(a Inf+ + b Inf-) with a + b = t + 3 (dimension t + 2), and two places Q1, Q2 of degree two. C_i is the subspace of the functions of V that vanish at Q_i (dimension t). The rows are all functions of C1 u C2 (2q^t - q^(t-2) of them, since C1 and C2 meet in dimension t - 2), and the columns are the rational points of X (16, 24 and 36 points). The pair Q1, Q2 is chosen so that on every t-set of columns one of the two blocks is an orthogonal array of index one. The arrays over q - 1 symbols are obtained by one step of the fusion lemma of Colbourn, Keri, Rivas Soriano and Schlage-Puchta (2010, Lemma 3.1), which removes two rows.
Built from: nothing (algebraic construction). The curve, the divisor, the basis of V, the places Q1, Q2 and the order of the columns are in the certificate files in certificates/ (the same files as genus2/certificates/ of supplement_C.zip in doi:10.5281/zenodo.24055371).
Program: programs/g2build.py in this folder (Python 3 with numpy): "python3 programs/g2build.py certificates/cert_g2_t{t}_q{q}_n{k}.json j CA_N_t_k_v.txt" with j = 0, or j = 1 for the fused array. It evaluates every function of C1 u C2 at the columns of the certificate (rows sorted by the coordinates of the function in V); the fusion step takes the zero function as rho1 and the next row as rho2 (Lemma 2.4 of the preprint). The same arrays (as sets of rows) are built by genus2/rank_check/mkarr.py of the deposit; the deposit also has CA(33271; 5, 16, 7) and CA(232897; 6, 16, 7) as explicit numpy files, which equal the files here up to the order of the rows.
