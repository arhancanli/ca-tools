Construction: Sherwood covering perfect hash families of strength 3 (n x k arrays of points of the affine plane AG(2, q), every vector with first coordinate 1), each giving CA(n(q^3 - q) + q; 3, k, q) by the theorem of Sherwood, Martirosyan and Colbourn (2006). Each family was grown column by column by a randomized search: a new column is a point in every row such that every set of three columns keeps a row in which its points are not on one line; when no column fits after a number of tries, the column with the fewest conflicting pairs is added and old columns covering those pairs are removed. The families are SCPHF(14; 154375, 5^2, 3), SCPHF(11; 137618, 7^2, 3), SCPHF(10; 116455, 8^2, 3), SCPHF(8; 134368, 13^2, 3), SCPHF(7; 106533, 17^2, 3) and SCPHF(7; 160839, 19^2, 3).

Built from: the 100,000-column families of the same q and n that entered with pull request #51 (table/certificates/CPHF_14_100000_5_3.txt, CPHF_11_100000_7_3.txt, CPHF_10_100000_8_3.txt, CPHF_8_100000_13_3.txt, CPHF_7_100000_17_3.txt, CPHF_7_100000_19_3.txt), given to the search as its start (PQ_START); since the search can remove columns, the new families do not contain them unchanged.

Program: https://github.com/arhancanli/quantavane-covering-arrays/blob/a31a18e/code/search/pq_cphf3big_v3.c (MIT; built with `cc -O3 -march=native -o pq_cphf3big pq_cphf3big_v3.c -lm`), run as `PQ_START=START pq_cphf3big q n 1000000 40000 SEED OUT 3600 3 16` (at most 1,000,000 columns, 40,000 CPU seconds, stop after 3,600 seconds without a new best, at most 3 conflicting pairs for a column added that way, 16 tries per column), with SEED 1005, 1007, 1008, 1013, 1017 and 1019 for q = 5, 7, 8, 13, 17 and 19. The searches stopped on the time limit (q = 13, 19) or after 3,600 seconds without a new best (the others), so a rerun with the same seed may stop at another width; the family files here are the certificates. The families were converted from our coding of GF(q) to the Conway coding of tools/cphf/cphfcheck.c. Every family was checked exactly three times on all triples of columns: by our checker pq_scphf3_bigcheck.c on our coding, again by it on each file here in the Conway field, and by fullcheck3.c, a second checker written separately from the search and from pq_scphf3_bigcheck.c (both at github.com/arhancanli/quantavane-covering-arrays/tree/a31a18e/checker).
