Construction: a Sherwood covering perfect hash family of strength 6 (an n x k array of points of AG(5, q), every vector with first coordinate 1), giving CA(n(q^6 - q) + q; 6, k, q) by the theorem of Sherwood, Martirosyan and Colbourn (2006). Every row r is a progression: column j holds the point gamma^(e_r j) of GF(q^5) = GF(q)[x]/(f), seen as a vector of GF(q)^5; the polynomial f, gamma and the exponents e_r are in the header of the released family (github.com/arhancanli/quantavane-covering-arrays, families/CA_11160081_6_5000_9.cphf.txt). This certificate is its first 4050 columns, so that its exact check fits the GitHub budget (every column subset of a covering perfect hash family is one).

Built from: nothing (the field GF(q^5), gamma and the exponents); the first 3950 columns are the certificate of submissions/canli-2026-11-cphf-t6-k5000-4, already in the table.

Program: https://github.com/arhancanli/quantavane-covering-arrays/blob/a31a18e/code/search/cphf6c_v2.c (MIT), run as `cphf6c 9 21 10 5 5000 21` (the pure cyclic mode, C = n: every row a progression, no search, so the run does not depend on time; rerunning it reproduced the family byte for byte on 7 November 2026), then converted to the Conway coding of tools/cphf/cphfcheck.c (for q = 9 by the field isomorphism between our polynomial x^2 + 1 and the Conway polynomial x^2 + 2x + 2, checked on every pair). The family was checked exactly at 5,000 columns by our checker scphf6_fast.c, and again by fullcheck6.c, a second checker written separately from the search and from scphf6_fast.c, on every base set whose largest column is at least 3950; with the table's own exact check of the first 3950 columns (submissions/canli-2026-11-cphf-t6-k5000-4) and the shift invariance of the progression rows (certified by fullcheck6 for every row) that decides every set of six columns.
